THE BRIEF IN ONE LINE
Think like McKinsey. Work like Google. Care like Unilever.
If a design decision is unclear, test it against this line. McKinsey gives us authority through ideas. Google gives us clarity, speed and a playful use of colour. Unilever gives us purpose, warmth and real human stories. KoKreaters sits where the three meet.

SECTION 1 · WHO WE ARE
1. Brand foundations
Positioning
KoKreaters is an independent technical and strategy consultancy for food, feed, non-food and specialty-ingredient businesses. It is founded and led by Ganeaysh Kulkarni, who brings 25 years of hands-on leadership in edible oils, specialty fats and food ingredients at Wilmar and Cargill, across India, Indonesia, Ukraine, Türkiye and the Netherlands.
What makes KoKreaters different is range. Most consultants are either plant engineers or strategists. KoKreaters works from the plant floor to the boardroom: designing a product, fixing a process, passing a customer audit, valuing an acquisition and writing the business plan, all with the same person who has done it inside global companies.
POSITIONING STATEMENT
KoKreaters helps food, nutrition and specialty-ingredient companies design better products, run better plants and make bolder decisions, by co-creating with their teams from the plant floor to the boardroom.

The name and the tagline
Ko stands for together: co-creation, collaboration, community. Kreaters are the people who make things happen. In Ganeaysh’s words, the work has “co-creation and creativity at its soul, at a global level”. The tagline in the logo spells out the method: Connect · Collaborate · Create.
Spelling is deliberate. Always “KoKreaters”: capital K, capital K, “-eaters” ending. Never “KoCreators”, “Kokreaters” or “Ko-Kreaters”. Add it to the CMS spell-check dictionary so editors are not prompted to “correct” it.
Trademark. A trademark application for KoKreaters is being filed. Use ™ after the name in the footer only. Do not use ® until registration is granted. [CONFIRM: filing status and when ® may be used]
Protect the name online. Consider also registering the common misspellings kocreaters.com and kokreators.com and redirecting them to kokreaters.com.

SECTION 2 · LOOK AND FEEL
2. The aura: McKinsey × Google × Unilever
The site should feel as credible as a top-tier consultancy, as easy as a Google product, and as human as a purpose-led consumer company. Borrow principles, not looks: nothing should be copied from any of these sites, and none of their names, logos or assets may appear on ours.
Reference
What we borrow
How it shows up on KoKreaters
What we avoid
McKinsey
Authority through ideas
Ideas as the product. Editorial serif headlines. Insight articles that lead the homepage. Confident restraint and lots of white space. One deep anchor colour.
Elegant serif headlines (Fraunces). Imperial Purple and Sapphire as the deep anchors. An Insights section with long-form articles, pull quotes and numbered “Exhibit” charts in brand colours.
Cold, grey, corporate tone. Walls of text. Consultant jargon.
Google
Clarity and play
Radical simplicity. Speed. Colour dots as a friendly signal. Rounded, tactile components. Helpful micro-interactions. Accessibility built in.
The coloured nodes in our logo play the role of Google’s dots: each expertise pillar owns one colour for wayfinding. Pill buttons, 16 px card corners, fast pages, clear focus states.
Toy-like visuals. Too many bright colours at once. Flat, characterless layouts.
Unilever
Purpose and warmth
A logo built from meaningful symbols. Rich, warm colour. Stories about everyday impact. Real people and real products.
Our logo is a story of connections, and the site animates it. Rich jewel tones (violet, orchid, saffron). Real photography of people, ingredients and plants. Impact stories told as outcomes.
Consumer-brand cuteness. Vague sustainability claims. Stock-photo clichés.

The blend

Five design principles
Light first, rich accents. Mostly white and soft Cloud backgrounds, with deep Imperial Purple bands for emphasis and jewel colours used sparingly, like accessories on a well-cut suit.
Ideas lead. Every page opens with a point of view, not a list of services.
Show the network. The node-and-line motif from the logo connects sections, illustrates the method and animates the hero. It is the visual signature.
Proof over adjectives. Numbers, places and outcomes instead of “world-class” and “cutting-edge”.
Effortless. Any visitor should reach the contact form in two clicks from anywhere, on any device.

RULES
Accessibility rules for colour
Body text: Midnight Ink or Graphite only.
Coloured text on white: Sapphire, Cobalt, Imperial, Royal Violet, Amethyst, Mauve and Orchid pass AA for normal text.
Never use Saffron, Sky or any tint as text on a light background. Saffron text is welcome on Imperial Purple or Midnight Ink.
Never rely on colour alone to carry meaning (for example pillar tags also carry the pillar name).

3.4  The node motif: our visual signature
The logo’s network of connected nodes is the brand’s most distinctive asset. Build a small graphic system from it and use it throughout.
Element
How to use it
Hero network
An SVG or canvas network of 12 to 20 ringed nodes in the core colours, joined by Sapphire lines. Nodes drift slowly; lines draw in as the page loads; the cursor gently attracts nearby nodes. Pause when off-screen. Static image when the visitor prefers reduced motion. Keep under 150 KB.
Connector lines
Thin Sapphire lines with a node at each end link sections down the page (for example from the method steps into the impact cards). Draw in on scroll.
Ringed bullets
Replace standard list bullets with small ringed nodes (8 px circle, 2 px white ring, pillar colour).
Soft discs
The three translucent overlapping discs (Lavender, Ice, Butter at 60–70% opacity) as background shapes behind heroes, portraits and stats.
Method diagram
Connect → Collaborate → Create as three large ringed nodes joined by the rising arrow.
Rising arrow
The swoosh under the wordmark becomes the underline for key words and the hover animation on text links.
Loader and favicon
The node cluster, nodes lighting up in sequence.

3.6  Icons and illustration
Use Google’s Material Symbols Rounded (free, Apache 2.0) at weight 300, 24 px grid, or a custom set drawn to the same rules: 1.75 px stroke, round caps and joins, a single small node dot as the accent. Icons take their pillar colour. Illustrations, where needed, are built from the node motif and soft discs, not from people cartoons.
3.7  Motion
Purposeful and calm: 150–300 ms, ease-out. Nothing bounces.
Sections fade up 16 px as they enter the viewport, once only.
Big numbers count up when first seen.
The arrow swoosh draws under key words; connector lines draw between sections.
Honour prefers-reduced-motion: switch every animation to a simple fade or none.
