Verdict in brief
Version 3 is a strong, credible base: the structure, the six services, the four ways to
engage and the nine impact stories already read like a serious consultancy. It does not yet
feel like 25 years of senior work, for five reasons, and fixing those five gives most of the
gain.

What already works (keep it): the navy-and-saffron palette, the Bodoni Moda and
Manrope pairing, the rapeseed hero, the six services with "Typical deliverables", the
challenge / what was done / result format on the Impact page, the career timeline, the free
3-MCPD playbook and the "Confidential by default" line.

The five changes with the biggest impact:

   1. Bring back the original logo, refined (Option A, approved). Network nodes,
      overlapping circles, the rising arrow and the tagline "Connect · Collaborate · Create",
      redrawn to consulting standard (next section).
   2. Stop the site sounding new. The line "From our founder's 25-year career, before
      KoKreaters" appears under almost every figure on the site. Said once, it is honest; said
      ten times, it tells visitors the firm itself has no track record. Say it once, in one
      footnote style, and let the 25 years lead.
   3. Put the person and the proof above the fold. The founder photo, name and "25 years ·
      8 sites · 5 countries · 3 regions" sit near the bottom of the Home page. A buyer of
      senior consulting buys the person: show him in the first screen.
   4. Add the proof a 25-year expert would have: scale numbers (plants, tonnes, teams,
       value), recognitions, talks and publications, professional memberships, and two or
       three named or anonymised client quotes.
   5. Make contact effortless. A "Book a 30-minute call" calendar link, a visible top
      navigation on desktop and a contact button in the header on every page.

The page-by-page list below carries everything else, including six small bugs to fix first.

Make 25 years felt
The site should read as a senior practice built on 25 years of work, and today it reads as a
new firm with an experienced founder. These changes move it, without overstating
anything.

1. Lead with the experience, not the firm's age.

       Hero eyebrow: "25 years · Agri, food and feed · Plant floor to boardroom".
       Replace every "From our founder's 25-year career, before KoKreaters" with one small
       footnote at the end of the Impact section: "Results delivered by Ganeaysh Kulkarni in
       senior roles at global agri-food companies. Client and employer details are kept
       confidential."
       Write "we" for the practice and "Ganeaysh" for results, consistently. Never show a
       founding year on the site (the footer copyright year is fine).

2. Show the person in the first screen. Add a small founder card in the hero, or a band
directly under it: portrait, "Ganeaysh Kulkarni, Founder and Principal", one line "25 years
leading R&D, plants and food safety across 5 countries and 3 continents", and a "Book a
call" button. Senior clients buy a person first.
