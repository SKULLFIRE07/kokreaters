Logo and removing AI references
Use the v4 logo everywhere: header, footer, favicon, social share image and email signature. Retire the v2 logo and the v3 “K” wordmark completely.
Nothing on the site should mention AI. Specifically:
Remove the chip “AI-assisted formulation” from “Where we help now”. Replace it with “Whipped toppings, creams and dairy alternatives”.
Remove the floating “Ask KoKreaters” button. It reads as a chatbot. If a floating button is wanted, use “Book a call” or a WhatsApp link instead.
Search all pages, menus, metadata and alt text for “AI”, “artificial intelligence”, “machine learning” and “digital twin”, and remove them.
Making it read as food
The site should say clearly that KoKreaters works on finished foods, not only on oils, fats, bakery and chocolate. Four changes do this.
1. A new homepage band: “Where fats meet food”. Place it right after “What we do”. A horizontal row of warm, appetising food photographs, each with a short label, scrollable on mobile.
Headline: Where fats meet food.
Line under it: Oils and fats decide the taste, texture, mouthfeel and shelf life of almost every food. We work from the fat blend to the finished product on the shelf.
Tiles: Whipped toppings and creams · Non-dairy creamers · Bakery margarines and shortenings · Chocolate, compounds and coatings · Fillings and spreads · Ice cream and frozen desserts · Frying oils and snacks · Mayonnaise, dressings and sauces · Indian sweets and ghee alternatives · Plant-based foods
Button: Discuss your application
2. Industries grow from six tiles to eight, in a clean 4 × 2 grid:
Agri and oilseeds
Edible oils and specialty fats
Bakery and food service
Cocoa, chocolate and confectionery
Dairy alternatives, toppings and creams (new): whipped toppings, non-dairy creamers, cooking creams, ice cream fats, cheese analogues
Culinary and prepared foods (new): frying and snacks, sauces and dressings, ready meals, Indian sweets and namkeen
Nutrition and specialty ingredients
Feed and animal nutrition
Each new tile needs its own industry page with the same layout as the existing ones. For dairy alternatives, toppings and creams, the page should cover fat blend and emulsifier design, overrun and foam stability, freeze-thaw and piping performance, homogenisation, UHT and aeration scale-up, and cost-in-use.
3. Food in the words, not only in the pictures.
Hero eyebrow: Techno-commercial consulting · Oils, fats and food
Product development text: New foods and reformulation, from whipped toppings and fillings to bakery fats and chocolate. Bench to launch, with cost-in-use.
Browser title: KoKreaters | Oils, Fats and Food Product Consulting
4. Image balance. About half the homepage photographs should be finished food (a whipped-cream dessert, croissant lamination, chocolate snap, frying, mithai), and half field, plant and lab. Use natural light and real food, with no generic stock smiles. Optionally, the hero can cross-fade slowly through three images: rapeseed field, plant, finished dessert, under the line From field, to plant, to plate.

One founder title everywhere: Founder and Principal, KoKreaters · Former Global Product & Process Design Director, Cargill · Chief Catalyst. Retire “Chief Catalyst and Founder” from v2.
One set of figures everywhere: use the v4 numbers. Remove the v2 line “14 years in oils, fats and food ingredients”.
