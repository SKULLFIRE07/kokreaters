## What we collect

We collect only what you enter yourself:

- in the contact form: your name, company, role, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, country, area of interest, message, and how you heard about us;

## Why we use it, and the legal basis

We use these details to reply to your enquiry or to send you the newsletter. We do this on the basis of your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to [office@kokreaters.com](mailto:office@kokreaters.com).
