## What we collect

We collect only what you enter yourself:

- in the contact form: your name, company, role, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, country, area of interest, message, and how you heard about us;
- in the newsletter form (The KoKreaters Note): your email address, and the record that you ticked the consent box and confirmed your subscription;
- in the playbook download form: your name, company, email address and country, and whether you also asked for The KoKreaters Note.

## Why we use it, and the legal basis

We use these details to reply to your enquiry, to send you the newsletter, or to send you the playbook you asked for. We do this on the basis of your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to [office@KoKreaters.com](mailto:office@kokreaters.com).

The newsletter uses double opt-in: you are subscribed only after you confirm your address from the email we send you. Every issue has a link to unsubscribe in one click.

## Who processes it

Your details are handled by the form service <Confirm id={9} note="form provider" /> and by our email provider (Google Workspace). The newsletter is sent with Brevo, an email service based in the European Union (France), which stores your address and your subscription for us. We share your details with no other third parties, and we never sell personal data.

## The playbook download

When you download a KoKreaters playbook, we use your name, company, email address and country to send you the playbook and, as the form says, to contact you about it. The legal basis is your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)), given with the tick box on the form. The newsletter is a separate choice: we add you to it only if you tick its own box and confirm your subscription.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. To use any of these rights, write to [office@KoKreaters.com](mailto:office@kokreaters.com). You also have the right to complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
