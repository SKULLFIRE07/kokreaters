KoKreaters website redesign – developer guide
20 November 2026 · Ganeaysh Kulkarni


What changes

The site keeps its structure and content but gets a new look: a darker “Delft blue” palette, new fonts, the real
KoKreaters logo, food and agri photos in place of the petrochemical refinery, and calmer, static sections without
blur-in effects.

         Homepage design: KoKreaters_Homepage_Design.html (open in any browser) and
         KoKreaters_Homepage_Design.png (full-page picture). This is the target layout.
         Style guide: KoKreaters_Style_Guide.html and .png list every colour, font size and button.
         Current site: kokreaters.com (Astro).
         Logo: use the full-colour KoKreaters logo from the business card (network mark plus “Connect ·
         Collaborate · Create”), the file kokreaters-logo.jpg in the digital-card repository. Export it as a transparent
         PNG or SVG for the header.

In the digital-card repository, use_this_logo.png and logo.svg are Cargill logos, not KoKreaters ones. Do not use
them; they are best deleted.

Layout and component rules

      Grid: content max width 1240 px, 40 px side padding (20 px on mobile), 12 columns.
      Spacing: 104–120 px above and below each section on desktop, 64–72 px on mobile.
      Header: white, logo 52 px tall, navigation visible on desktop (Expertise, Industries, Approach, Insights,
      About) plus a Deep Delft “Start a conversation” button. The hamburger menu is for mobile only.
      Buttons: square corners, no shadows, 15–16 px padding. Primary = Deep Delft fill; secondary = Deep
      Delft outline; on dark = white fill or white outline; saffron fill with navy text for one CTA per page only.
      Labels: a 40 × 2 px saffron line before section labels on dark backgrounds; the lead statistic gets a 3 px
      saffron top rule, the others a 1 px line.
      Photos: square corners, object-fit: cover. Wherever text sits on a photo, add a navy gradient
      (rgba(14,31,77,0.92) fading to transparent).
      Motion: remove the blur-in and fade-in effects on scroll. Content shows the moment the page loads. A
      gentle hover (underline on links, slight image zoom on tiles) is enough.
     “Ask KoKreaters” chat button: keep it if it is useful, but make it smaller and Deep Delft, so it does not
     compete with the main buttons.
     Accessibility: text contrast at least 4.5:1, real <a> and <button> elements, alt text on every photo.


Homepage structure, section by section

The homepage runs in 13 sections, top to bottom; the attached homepage file shows each one at full size.

   1. Top bar (navy): red dot, “Latest insight” and a link to the newest article.
   2. Header (white): logo, visible navigation, “Start a conversation” button.
   3. Hero: rapeseed-field photo with a navy gradient on the left. Label “Techno-commercial consulting · Agri,
     food & feed”, title “Deep expertise, turned into products, plants and markets.”, intro text, two buttons, and
     a bottom line “Based in the Netherlands” plus the five countries.
   4. Track record (porcelain): 25 years in industry, 14 years in oils, fats and food ingredients, 5 countries,
      WHO recognition.
   5. What we do (white): six numbered services in a 3 × 2 grid with thin rules: product development; process
     development and plant performance; market access and strategy; food safety, quality and regulatory;
      deals, due diligence and partnerships; fractional CTO and Head of R&D.
   6. Our approach (ice): three tall photo cards, Develop (lab), Scale (process plant), Access (bulk carrier).
   7. Industries (white): six photo tiles (agri and oilseeds; edible oils and specialty fats; feed and animal
     nutrition; bakery and food service; cocoa, chocolate and confectionery; nutrition and specialty ingredients)
     plus a text line for the rest.
   8. Impact (navy): −25 °C refinery in Ukraine and the WHO recognition, in large Bodoni numbers.
  9. Insights (white): the two published articles and the bleaching-earth explainer as “coming soon”.
 10. Netherlands band: Dutch polder photo, “A Dutch base. A global reach.”
 11. Founder (porcelain): portrait, quote in italic, “Ganeaysh Kulkarni, Chief Catalyst and Founder, KoKreaters”.
 12. Call to action (Deep Delft): “Let’s create something together.”, saffron button, office@KoKreaters.com.
 13. Footer (near-black ink): logo on a white plate, link columns, contact, legal links.

Implementation checklist

Most of the change is a theme update in the existing Astro project, so it can ship in stages.

       Add the colour variables and switch all text from #1A1A1A to Delft Navy
       Replace Source Serif 4 and Inter with Bodoni Moda and Manrope (self-hosted with Fontsource)
       Replace the header logo with the business-card KoKreaters logo; show the desktop navigation
       Rebuild the hero with the rapeseed photo, the navy gradient and the new copy
       Remove the blur-in and fade-in scroll effects
       Rebuild What we do (six services), Our approach and Industries with the new photos
       Add the Netherlands band and update the founder section (Chief Catalyst title)
       Apply the content fixes above
       Restyle buttons, CTA band and footer
       Check every page at 390 px (phone) and 1440 px wide, and run a Lighthouse check for speed and contrast
       Carry the same style through the expertise, industry, insight and about pages
