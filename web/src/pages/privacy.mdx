## What we collect

We collect only what you enter yourself:

- in the contact form: your name, email address, company and message, and, if you choose to give them, your role, phone or WhatsApp number, country, area of interest and how you heard about us;
- in the newsletter form (The KoKreaters Note): your email address, and the record that you ticked the consent box and confirmed your subscription;
- in the playbook download form: your name, company, email address and country, and whether you also asked for The KoKreaters Note.

## Why we use it, and the legal basis

We use the details from the contact form only to reply to your enquiry. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before a possible agreement, and our legitimate interest in answering the people who write to us (GDPR Article 6(1)(b) and (f)).

We send the newsletter and the playbook on the basis of your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)), given with the tick box on those forms. You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to [office@KoKreaters.com](mailto:office@kokreaters.com).

The newsletter uses double opt-in: you are subscribed only after you confirm your address from the email we send you. Every issue has a link to unsubscribe in one click.

## Who processes it

We use these processors to handle your details for us:

- **Vercel** (Vercel Inc., United States) hosts the website and runs the forms; like any web host it processes your IP address to deliver the pages.
- **Resend** (Resend Inc., United States) delivers the messages you send with the contact and playbook forms to our inbox, and sends you the confirmation email. If Resend cannot deliver a message, it is passed to **FormSubmit** (formsubmit.co) instead, which forwards it to our inbox.
- **Google Workspace** (Google Ireland Ltd.) is our email provider, where your message is stored and answered.
- **Brevo** (Sendinblue SAS, France) sends the newsletter and stores your address and your subscription for us.

The newsletter is a separate choice: we add you to it only if you tick its own box and confirm your subscription.

You have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. You also have the right to complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
